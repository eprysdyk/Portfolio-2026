Eric Rysdyk

Architectural Technologist

I am an Architectural Technology graduate from CDI College with practical experience in residential and commercial construction documentation. My work focuses on preparing accurate drawing sets for multi-disciplinary consultants, coordinating documentation packages, and ensuring projects meet building code and municipal requirements.

I am passionate about the technical side of architecture—the details that make a design buildable. I enjoy the challenge of taking a concept and translating it into clear, precise drawings that contractors and consultants can rely on.

Currently, I am expanding my skills in Revit and BIM, and I am always looking for opportunities to learn, grow, and contribute to meaningful projects.

When I'm not drafting, you'll find me camping, woodworking, fishing, or tinkering with computer builds. I believe the best work comes from curiosity and a willingness to keep learning.
